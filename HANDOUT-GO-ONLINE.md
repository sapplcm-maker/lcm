# Put the Ministry Portal Online — 15-Minute Job, No Experience Needed

**Please give this to any student, youth leader, parish secretary, or tech-savvy friend.**
The portal software is already finished and tested. This handout is only about getting it a permanent home on the internet.

---

## What you need
- The file **lcm-ministry-portal.zip** (289 KB)
- A computer or phone with internet
- About 15 minutes

## Step 1 — Make a free account at Replit
1. Open **https://replit.com**
2. Click **Sign up** — use any email address. (Free; no card needed.)
3. Keep this email + password in a safe place — it is the ministry's account now.

## Step 2 — Create the project
1. Click the green **+ Create** button (top right).
2. Choose the **Node.js** template.
3. Name it **lcm-portal** → **Create Repl**.

## Step 3 — Upload the portal files
1. On your computer, **unzip** `lcm-ministry-portal.zip` (right-click → Extract All).
2. In Replit's left **Files** panel, click **⋯** (three dots) → **Upload file** — or simply **drag the files** in.
3. Upload the **contents** of the unzipped folder: the `server`, `public`, and `docs` folders plus `package.json`.
4. Make sure `package.json` is in the TOP-LEVEL file list (not inside another folder).

## Step 4 — Run it
1. Click the green **Run** button at the top.
2. Wait ~30 seconds. The portal creates its own database automatically.
3. A web address appears — e.g. `https://lcm-portal.yourname.repl.co`. **That is the permanent link.**
4. Send the link to all members. They open it from anywhere — even with the parish office computer turned off.

## Step 5 — First login
- Sign in as **admin / Admin@123**, then **immediately** change the password:
  Profile → Account Settings → Security.
- Add real members under **Members** (side menu).

## Notes
- Free Replit sites "nap" after ~15 minutes without visitors. The first person to open the link wakes it in about 30 seconds — data is always saved. This is normal for free hosting.
- The portal is secure: passwords are encrypted, logins are limited, and evaluation results stay confidential until the administrator approves them.
- If anything goes wrong at any step, note the exact words on the screen — the person who set up the system (Accio) can fix it from a description.

## If Replit is not an option
- **Glitch.com** — very similar steps (free account → New Project → upload → Run).
- **Office computer** — run `PUBLISH-NOW-WINDOWS.bat` from the zip on the parish PC: creates a public link for as long as the PC stays on (good for office hours).
- **Permanent paid** (needs a card + a helper): ~$5/month server (see README appendix).
